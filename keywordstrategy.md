## Operating rules

- One page = one primary keyword cluster. Never two pages chasing the same phrase.
- City pages answer "who do I hire"; blog posts answer "how do I fix" — internal-link the two.
- Watch Search Console → Performance monthly: any query with impressions but low clicks is
  a title/description rewrite opportunity; send me a screenshot and I'll rewrite them.
